How it maps to dls_pack


  Output is a GMWB blob: 44-byte header, then instruments (12 B),
  regions (80 B), waves (12 B), and the PCM block. µ-law halves the PCM at the
  cost of ~38 dB SNR. Coefficients (EG1/EG2/LFO/pan) are baked at the chosen
  rate so the device never does float math, exactly as the C packer does.
  


  Rounding mirrors the C tool: PCM uses round-to-nearest-ties-to-even
  (lrint), all baked coefficients use round-half-away-from-zero
  (llround/lround). Verified byte-for-byte identical to
  build/dls_pack.exe and build/dls_pack_mulaw.exe on
  GM.DLS @ 22050 Hz (3,207,408 B 16-bit, 1,668,026 B µ-law).
